Research

From atomic-scale surfaces to working devices

We study catalytic materials together with their reaction kinetics and mechanisms, linking the chemistry of a surface to the performance of a full cell, reactor or membrane.

Electrochemical energy storage

Redox flow batteries for storing solar and wind power at scale: functionalized carbon electrodes, vanadium redox kinetics, semi-solid flow cells and electrochemical nanofluids.

Learn more →

Catalysis for green energy and chemicals

Heterogeneous catalysts for hydrogen production and CO2 conversion, built on metal–ceramic core–shell architectures that tailor surface chemistry together with mass and heat transport.

Learn more →

Advanced functional materials

Nanostructured oxides and hybrid materials by design: heteroatom-doped CeO2 nanocubes that scavenge reactive oxygen radicals, surface-functionalized nanoparticles for electrochemical nanofluids, and core–shell and MOF architectures.
